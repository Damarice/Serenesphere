Experience Kenya

10 Days of Culture, Adventure & Discovery

Immerse yourself in the vibrant heart of East Africa. From Nairobi's urban energy to the Maasai Mara's untamed wilderness.

Your Journey Awaits

Day by Day Itinerary
Day 01

Nairobi

  • Guests arrive at JKIA (3:00am/4:00am)
  • Breakfast & Lunch at the hotel
  • Meet and greet (12noon or 5:00pm)
  • Soft jazz music/other entertainment
  • Dinner at the hotel
Day 02

Nairobi → Nyeri

  • Visit Nairobi Museum
  • Visit Giraffe Center
  • Tour Ubuntu Kreative Village
  • Lunch at Ubuntu
  • Afternoon drive to Nyeri (1–2 activities)
  • Evening campfire with storytelling
Day 03

Nyeri → Kisumu → Homabay → Rusinga

  • Check in at ecolodges around Mt. Kenya
  • Forest walks
  • Kikuyu/Slave history tour
  • Lunch in Nyeri
  • Flight to Kisumu, transfer to Homabay & Rusinga
Day 03 & 04

Rusinga

  • Fishing and bird watching
  • Cooking local food onsite
  • Tom Mboya history site
  • Local women craft making
  • Luo dancers (Dudu/Ramogi)
  • Visit Suba cultural center
Day 04

Nandi

  • Transfer to Nandi
  • Lunch in Nandi
  • Meet athletes & train/jog together
  • Dinner in Nandi
Day 05

Great Rift Valley

  • Tea party along Kericho plantations
  • Visit Lake Nakuru
  • Lunch in Nakuru
  • Check in Naivasha
  • Dinner & bonfire night
Day 06

Maasai Mara

  • Early transfer to Mara
  • Check in at Salt Lake Eco Lodge
  • Evening safari drive
Day 07

Maasai Mara

  • Early safari drive & bush breakfast
  • Hot air balloon safari
  • Lunch at the lodge
  • Visit Maasai village & Dumu dance
  • Photography & birding safari
  • Bonfire night
Day 08

Nairobi → Watamu

  • Drive back to Nairobi, Rift escarpment stopover
  • Shopping for local crafts
  • Transfer to Watamu
  • Beach rest, henna painting, madafu drinks
  • Dinner by the beach
Day 09

Watamu

  • Morning yoga at the beach
  • Lake conservation project
  • Farm-to-table lunch
  • Evening rest & massage therapy (optional)
  • Boat rides, sunset/star gazing
  • Live taarab music/band
Day 10

Farewell

  • Late breakfast/lunch
  • Guest debrief & gift hampers
  • Transfer to SGR/airport
  • Flight to Nairobi → connection to US
