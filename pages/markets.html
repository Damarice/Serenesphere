Wholesale Inquiries

We invite ethical retailers, wellness centers, and cultural institutions to partner with us in spreading the message of Stillness as Sovereignty. Our wholesale program offers a curated selection of high-value, ethically sourced artifacts and wellness products. By partnering with us, you are not just acquiring unique goods; you are directly investing in a model of Regeneration Over Extraction, ensuring fair compensation and cultural sovereignty for our community co-creators.

Learn the principles of Rooted Living, nervous system regulation, and intentional disconnection from anywhere in the world, preparing you for a deeper immersive experience or sustaining your transformation long after your return.
