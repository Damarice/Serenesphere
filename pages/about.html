About Company

We are not a travel company; we are a Regenerative Movement.

Serenesephere specializes in Serenity-Centered Journeys that offer a radical alternative to conventional tourism. In a world that demands speed, we sell slowness. In a world of noise, we sell peace. Our mission is to create immersive, community-led experiences that reconnect travelers to the rhythms of the earth, the wisdom of ancestors, and the stillness within themselves—while regenerating the land, culture, and economies of African communities.

Our Team

Our Amazing Team Players

Our team is composed of cultural translators, strategic leaders, local operations experts, and a circle of elite advisors including herbalists, elders, and therapists. We are co-creators of the Serenity-Centered Global Framework, dedicated to spreading the message of Vitality Regeneration and Rooted Living.

Location: USA

Key Roles:

  • Primary lead for major investors, international partnerships, and the diaspora community.
  • Fosters the company culture and ensures all teams are aligned with the core mission.

Jochebed Emali

Chief Operating Officer (COO)
