Serenesephere Chapters are local and global communities dedicated to cultural regeneration, wellness, and rooted living. Join a chapter near you or help establish one in your region.

US Programs

Our US Programs serve as vital hubs for the Serenity-Centered movement, bringing the principles of Vitality Regeneration and Rooted Living to the Western context. These programs include introductory workshops, local Council Circles, and integration intensives designed to support participants before and after their immersive journeys to Africa. They are also essential for Diaspora travelers, offering pre-trip ancestry coaching and community-building events focused on identity reclamation and intergenerational healing.

Sister Cities Programs

The Sister Cities Programs are a core component of our global expansion, ensuring that the Serenity-Centered model evolves beyond a mere retreat into a global movement. These initiatives create direct, reciprocal relationships between communities in Africa and civic wellness initiatives worldwide. We provide toolkits and mentorship to empower global partners to replicate the framework, fostering connection, cultural exchange, and shared prosperity through balance. This is how we build a network of Regeneration Over Extraction across continents.
