Frequently Asked Questions

"No phone? Really? What about emergencies?"

We have emergency protocols in place. The detox is intentional and important for your transformation.

"What are the accommodations like?"

Clean, safe, and intentionally simple. Think comfortable beds and starry nights, not flat-screen TVs.

"Is this safe?"

Yes. We have detailed safety measures and strong community relationships.

"I'm not African. Is this for me?"

All are welcome who come with humility and a desire to learn.

"I'm part of the Diaspora but feel disconnected. Will I fit in?"

This journey is designed for you. Feeling disconnected is part of the path home. You will be held with grace.

"What should I pack?"

Pack for presence, not for pictures.
